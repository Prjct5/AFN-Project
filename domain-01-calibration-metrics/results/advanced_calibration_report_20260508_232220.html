📉 Technical Definitions

  • Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC): MCC = (TP×TN - FP×FN) / √[(TP+FP)(TP+FN)(TN+FP)(TN+FN)]
    Uses all four confusion matrix quadrants. High score only if model predicts well on both positive and negative classes.
  • Expected Calibration Error (ECE): ECE = Σ (|B_m|/n) × |acc(B_m) - conf(B_m)|
    Weighted average of the gap between accuracy and confidence across M bins.
  • Brier Score: BS = (1/N) Σ (f_t - o_t)²
    Proper scoring rule measuring mean squared difference between predicted probability and actual outcome. Punishes confidently wrong predictions.
  • Bootstrapping: 1000 iterations with random subsampling to generate 95% confidence intervals. Proves results aren't a fluke of a specific test set.
